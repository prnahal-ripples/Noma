NOMA · design system

Bottom navigation — three versions

All three share the same geometry: 16px inset on left, right and bottom, and a 0.8× shrink floor. Scroll inside any phone to contract its bar, or use the control below to contract all three at once.

Version 1

Icon only

The baseline, with the pill spacing corrected — the selected pill now sits 8px from every edge of the bar instead of 8px top/bottom and 15px left/right.

Version 2

Icon + label

Each tab carries its name. On contract the labels both scale down and fade out, and their row collapses — so the bar genuinely gets shorter rather than keeping empty space.

Version 3

Icon only + AI button

A fifth action sits outside the bar on the right. The bar gives up that width, so its four tabs sit closer together. Green here is a mint→lime ramp with an ink glyph — never a flat fill, never white on green.

393 × 600 frames — iPhone 15 logical width, shortened so three fit on one screen. Each phone runs the same demo.html; only its ?v= differs.
All three contract to the same silhouette: 51.2px tall at 80% width.
