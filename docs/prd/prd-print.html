2Problem and insight

You can't see what you breathe.Air Week One

An air purifier is a box that hums. When it works, nothing visibly happens. The source material is blunt about the consequence:

Invisible protection that runs silently is, to the user, indistinguishable from a switched-off box.Air Week One — the "away-mode trap"

In Indian homes this compounds. People switch purifiers off to save electricity — losing the benefit they paid for — because the cost is visible and the protection is not. 82% of Indians cannot see indoor air without a device.

The insight the product is built on

Air is invisible, so perceived value is whatever the app makes visible.

Perceived value is therefore a design output, not a hardware output. That single sentence justifies the entire seven-day arc in §9, the narration requirement in §8, and the existence of the energy card in §11. It is the most load-bearing line in the source material.

The supplied material describes two different products. Nothing says which ships first. This is the decision everything else waits on.

Recommendation Ship A, architected for B. The purifier story is the only part of the material that argues rather than asserts. This is the owner's call. Everything below is scoped to A unless marked B. Until it is ruled, teams will build against different assumptions.

4Critical blockers

Thirteen conflicts are open. These four are different in kind: each one stops work rather than slowing it. Full list in §24.

C-7 · The differentiator has no engineering model

AQI-weighted filter life is the single claim the product is built on, and it exists only as a worked example. The material states the effect ("above AQI 200 a filter absorbs 3–5× more particulate per hour") and shows Delhi at 38% vs Mumbai at 64% on identical runtime — but supplies no formula, no coefficients, and no validation data.

It gates F-FILTER-HEALTH, which is simultaneously the differentiator and the recurring-revenue lever. Nothing else in v1 is worth as much as closing this.

C-11 · "Data never leaves home" vs three networked features

The product claims on-device AI and DPDP compliance, then ships three features that cannot work locally: CPCB outdoor blending, forecast-driven pre-clean, and neighbourhood comparison. "Never leaves home" and "uses tomorrow's forecast" cannot both be literally true.

The claim most likely means personal and biometric data stays local; ambient public data is fetched — which is defensible and still a strong position. It must be narrowed to that before it reaches store copy or a privacy policy. It also blocks the analytics taxonomy, because an analytics SDK is by definition exfiltration.

C-8 · No safety class for agent autonomy

"Act silently" is offered per capability, but every example is comfort-grade (fan speed, pre-clean, reorder). Nothing prevents it applying to a lock, and Reorder filters at that level is an unattended purchase. Separately, the SpO₂-below-94% → purifier-Turbo trigger is a health-adjacent inference presented as an ordinary automation, with no medical or legal position.

v1 ships "Ask me" only until capabilities carry a safety class.

C-2 · The product and assistant have five names between them

Product: NOMA · Ding · Noise Home · Noise Air · Noise Sanctuary.
Assistant: Ding · Noise AI Agent · Sanctuary Assistant · unnamed first person.
Score: Home Score · Sanctuary Score · Air Score.

All user-facing copy, every i18n key, all persona prompts, the glossary and every SKU string are downstream. Highest-leverage single decision on the list.

7Product principles

From the name: ma (間), the intentional space between things, and no (野), nature and openness. The empty space is doing work — it is composed, not left over.

PrincipleTest
Whitespace is a featureIf a screen looks sparse that is likely correct. Density is the failure mode here, not a virtue.
One idea per screenThe primary action is findable in one glance.
Calm over informativeShow the state of things, not every metric that produced it. Detail on demand, never volunteered.
Never volunteer alarmUrgency is earned by real events, not by styling.
Relief, not delightThe emotional target is a glance that confirms everything is fine. Personality lives in small moments, never in the chrome.

Deliberately not

Dashboard-dense · blue-lit · alarm-forward · gamified. And never: make the accent green the ground; require app fluency for basic safety; use a raw metric as a headline; hide a trade-off.

8The agent model

This is not a feature bolted onto the app — it determines what the app is. Home is not a grid of devices; it is a list of what the agent has decided, is proposing, or has already done.

8.1 Three zones, in this order

#ZoneContains
1Settled nowWhat is already fine. Per-room readings.
2Needs your callProposals awaiting a decision, badged with a count.
3Done todayCompleted actions, each still undoable.

The ordering is load-bearing: reassurance, then the ask, then the receipt. Someone opening the app anxious gets "air is clean in 2 rooms" before they are handed a decision.

8.2 Graduated autonomy — per capability, three levels

The single most important mechanic. Autonomy is not global and not binary. It is granted one capability at a time and starts at the bottom.

LevelBehaviour
Ask me defaultAgent proposes. Nothing runs without a tap.
Act & tell meAgent acts, then reports. Undo remains available.
Act silentlyAgent acts without notifying.

Capabilities, each with an independent level: Adjust fan speed · Pre-clean before forecasts · Reorder filters.

Right now, I only ever propose. Nothing runs without your tap. As you approve things, you can let me act on my own — one capability at a time.the agent, in the prototype
Trust is promoted on evidence "You've accepted 7 fan suggestions in a row — ready to let this run itself?"
A consecutive-accept count, offered back as the reason. This makes a per-capability accept/reject tally application state, not analytics — a real data requirement falling out of a trust decision.

8.3 The explanation contract — four fixed slots

Every proposal exposes "Why this?" with the same four slots, always in the same order. This is a contract, not a copy template.

SlotJobExample from the prototype
SensedThe evidence"Outdoor AQI at 118 and climbing, forecast 182 by 10pm; bedroom holding at AQI 38 (PM2.5 9 µg/m³)"
DecidedThe action"Pre-clean 8:30–9:00 at medium, then hold windows-closed mode"
Instead ofThe counterfactual"Reacting at 11pm — which would run loud while you sleep"
Obeys ruleThe constraint respected"Quiet hours 11pm–7am stays intact"
Why this matters "Instead of" is the differentiating slot. Explaining what the agent chose not to do is what makes a decision read as considered rather than arbitrary — most assistant UIs ship the first two slots and stop. "Obeys rule" is a promise: naming the constraint it respected is how the agent proves it is bounded. Ship the disclosure without that slot and standing rules become unverifiable — the user has no way to know their rule held.

8.4 Rehearsal before execution

A proposal previews as a step-by-step timeline, each step individually editable. Nothing happens until you tap Run.

TimeStepDetailAction
8:30pmstart pre-cleanBedroom only · medium speed · ~30 minEdit
9:00pmwindows-closed modeRecirculate · hold until AQI dropsEdit
11:00pmrespect quiet hoursFan capped at 28 dB · your standing ruleLocked

Rule-derived steps render Locked, not Edit. A user cannot casually edit away their own constraint from inside a one-off plan. Rules change where rules live.

8.5 Standing rules — goals, not one-time actions

RuleValueCopy shown
Quiet hours11pm–7amreferenced by name in "Obeys rule"
Noise ceiling while asleep28 dB"Lower is quieter but cleans slower. 28 dB is about a whisper."
Target overnight PM2.5under 12"The goal I'll hold the room to, not a one-time action."

Note the copy discipline: each rule states its trade-off and anchors its unit to something human. That is part of the model, not decoration.

8.6 Promotion, reversal, isolation

  • "Do this always" promotes an accepted one-off into a standing automation — and appears on completed items too, because the moment someone appreciates what it did is the moment to offer the upgrade. The Automation tab then separates Created by your agent from Created by you.
  • Undo stays on completed actions, including autonomous ones.
  • Recurrence is offered contextually: "Smog nights repeat this season — do this automatically."
  • The chat thread is deliberately context-free: "Starting fresh — this thread does not carry over anything from Home." Ambient agent and conversational agent are separate sessions. Anyone reversing this should say why.
The most ambitious behaviour in any source The launch prototype shows the agent reasoning across devices in one turn — rescheduling the vacuum to 5:30am because yesterday's camera footage showed construction workers arriving at 8am, while answering a question about when Maa usually wakes. That is genuinely impressive and is the hardest thing to deliver under an on-device constraint (§16). Nothing in the material addresses how.

Unanswered questions this model raises

  • Where does the accept/reject tally live, and does one rejection reset the promotion counter?
  • What happens when a proposal's window passes un-actioned? "Later" and "Not tonight" exist; expiry behaviour does not.
  • Can two proposals conflict, and who arbitrates?
  • Is there a declined-consent state?

The structure to protect The night-hook → morning-proof pair (D1→D2) is the most important structure in the product. Night: "Tonight the AQI outside hits 200+. 12The differentiator

AQI-weighted filter life, not clock-counted hours. Competitors count runtime, so three months in Delhi's winter smog and three months in Mumbai's monsoon return the same filter score.

Every other purifier tells you your filter is 72% healthy — three months in a row. We don't. Because we actually measure it.Noise Sanctuary
Same filter, same hoursReal AQICompetitor saysNOMA says
Delhi NCR · winter28772%38%
Mumbai · monsoon9472%64%

Identical hardware, identical runtime, 26 points apart. That gap is the product. A Delhi filter can exhaust in 4 months what a Mysore filter takes 12 months to use up.

Method
AQI-weighted particulate load, computed hourly
Inputs
Live outdoor AQI · indoor particle sensor · fan runtime
Claimed effect
Above AQI 200, a filter absorbs 3–5× more particulate per hour
Alerting
Predictive — surfaces before failure, reorder timed to arrive before exhaustion
Spec status
None. No formula, no coefficients, no validation data
C-7 — the most serious gap in this document This is a marketing claim awaiting an engineering model. It is the one thing that makes the product defensible against Xiaomi at the same price, and it is specified nowhere. Closing it is worth more than any other v1 work.

16Privacy and compliance

Stated repeatedly and specifically across the prototypes. This is India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 — a legal obligation, not a marketing line.

What the product claims

  • On-device AI. "DPDP compliant. Your data never leaves home."
  • Face data is stored "only on your device. It is never uploaded to any server."
  • Face tagging is gated by an explicit consent sheet (Decline / Allow & Continue) citing the DPDP Act 2023 by name.
  • The camera event view is badged "No cloud · DPDP compliant."
  • The agent is labelled "On-device · Private" in the chat header.
C-11 · The unresolved core tension

Three shipped features cannot work locally:

  • Outdoor AQI blending — requires a CPCB network call
  • Forecast-driven pre-clean — requires tomorrow's forecast
  • Neighbourhood comparison — aggregated peer data, server-side by definition

"Never leaves home" and "uses tomorrow's forecast" cannot both be literally true. The claim most likely means personal and biometric data stays local; ambient public data is fetched — defensible, and still a strong position. It must be narrowed to that before it appears in store copy or a privacy policy.

Three consequences the prototypes do not resolve

  1. On-device inference bounds model size and latency, and therefore how clever the agent can be — including the cross-device reasoning in §8.6.
  2. Consent is per-feature, not per-app. Declining face recognition must leave a working camera. No declined state is designed anywhere.
  3. Telemetry is exfiltration. Whether analytics is exempt, separately consented, or aggregated on-device before sending is an architecture decision that must be made before the first event is named.

Also required and unaddressed: account deletion and data export are DPDP obligations with no surface in any prototype.

21Motion

ADR-005. Canonical: src/tokens/motion.tokens.js. Gates: .claude/rules/motion.md.

Bounce means "something happened"; stillness means "everything is fine."the rule that resolves playful-vs-ma

Motion is playful — springy, bouncy, Airbnb-register — on discrete moments (a tap, an arrival, a confirmation) and almost perfectly still on ambient ones (live readings, monitoring, charts). Calm is the ground; bounce is the event. That split is how owner-stated playfulness and the ma philosophy stop fighting.

TokenValueUse
durations80 · 140 · 240 · 360 · 480 mshover → tap → card → expander → sheet
durations.ambient4000 msThe only long value. Breathing ripples, idle pulses
easings.spring0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1~10% overshoot — the house spring. Buttons, chips, toggles, cards
easings.springBold0.22, 1.8, 0.36, 1Hero arrivals — sheets, overlays, toasts
easings.exit0.4, 0, 1, 1Every exit. Never bounces
stagger45 ms lists · 60 ms gridsBetween siblings entering
scalepress .96 · pop 1.04 · breathe 1.02breathe is the ambient ceiling

Registered patterns

MO-PRESS press & release · MO-POP arrival pop · MO-STAGGER list entrance · MO-SHEET sheet rise · MO-KNOB toggle thumb · MO-TOAST · MO-PULSE count change · MO-BREATHE ambient (the only looping one).

Non-negotiable gates

  1. Tokens only — UI code never contains a raw duration or bezier.
  2. prefers-reduced-motion is mandatory on every animation. An accessibility gate, no exceptions.
  3. Exits never bounce. Overshoot on the way out reads as broken, not playful.
  4. Ambient never springs. A live number does not bounce because it changed.
  5. Alarm never bounces. If something is wrong, motion gets out of the way.
Provenance note The owner referenced a bouncy style defined in an earlier session unavailable to this one. These values are a fresh codification of the stated direction (playful / fun / quirky / bouncy / Airbnb-like). If the earlier values surface, reconcile by revising ADR-005 — not by editing UI code.
