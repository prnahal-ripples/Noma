# Glossary

> **Canonical: `docs/voice/noma-voice-guide.md`** §5 (vocabulary) and §5.3
> (the product glossary). This file is a pointer, not a copy.

The banned list is organised as **families, not single words** (§5.2): form
voice, corporate distance, narrator, machine-writing tells, hype, shame, alarm,
insider, health overclaim, slang, and double meanings. A rule is not passed by
dodging the one example word.

The fixed product terms are §5.3:

- **W-01** PM2.5 in µg/m³ for anything Noma measured. **Never AQI indoors.**
  AQI is legal only quoting the outdoor public index, with source and age.
- **W-02** Modes are proper nouns: Auto, Turbo, Night, Away, Home.
- **W-03** Filters have a remaining *life*, not a health.
- **W-05** Rooms are called what the person named them.
- **W-14** A navigation label names a destination. It does not speak.

⚠ **`hi` terminology is still owed**, and the guide raises the bar rather than
lowering it: **L-02** says every language is a *peer, not a translation*, so
Hindi lines are re-derived, never converted. **M-21** fixes the Hindi
first-person as masculine. **D-1** (tum/aap) is a launch blocker.
